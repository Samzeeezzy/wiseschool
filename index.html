WHO WE ARE

Quality education.
Within every child's reach.

Wisdom International School of Excellence (WISE) is a high-quality, low-cost private school serving low- and medium-income communities in the Federal Capital Territory, Abuja.

Established on 16 September 2013, WISE began in a three-bedroom flat in FHA Lugbe with a commitment to expanding access to meaningful education. We nurture learners morally, socially and academically, preparing them with the knowledge and practical skills needed for today and the future.

HOW WE LEARN

Beyond textbooks.
Skills for life.

WISE uses the Nigerian National Curriculum as a foundation and enriches it with practical learning and the skills children need to thrive in the 21st century.

  • + Science, Technology, Reading, Engineering, Arts and Mathematics (STREAM)
  • + Soft skills and behavioural competencies
  • + Financial literacy and entrepreneurship
  • + ICT, vocational learning, sports and arts
  • + Group learning, teamwork and community projects
